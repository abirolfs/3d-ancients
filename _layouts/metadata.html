date Date ISO 8601 Period of Time Specification Mandatory, if applicable Repeats For a single, specific year, enter date in YYYY format, where YYYY is a four-digit, zero-padded number. For a time period, enter date in YYYY/YYYY format, with the earliest date before the /, and latest date after. Years B.C.E should be indicated by a "-" before the first digit.

  • 0100/0150

display_date Mandatory, if applicable Repeats For a single, specific year, enter date in YYYY format, where YYYY is a one to four-digit, non-padded number. For a time period, enter date in YYYY - YYYY format, with the earliest date before the -, and latest date after. At the end, indicate whether the date or date range is BCE or CE, unless the first date is BCE and the second date is CE. If that is the case, indicate BCE/CE after each year.
  • 40 - 20 BCE
  • 200 CE
  • 50 BCE - 50 CE
