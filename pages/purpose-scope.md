# 3D Ancients - Purpose and Scope
<br>
This digital collection was developed as part of an academic Capstone project at Indiana University Bloomington, for completion of the Master of Information Science (MIS) degree with a Digital Humanities specialization. **The primary purpose of the 3D Ancients collection is to provide access to a variety of virtual heritage efforts for the ancient Mediterranean.** In the context of this collection, the ancient Mediterranean includes any artifact, architecture, or site that was created between 3000 BCE and 500 CE in the geographic area of the Mediterranean Sea. Cultures that fit into this scope include (but are certainly not limited to) Classical Greece, Imperial Rome, Ancient Carthage, the Hittite Empire, and Ancient Egypt.

**Virtual heritage can be defined as the utilization of interactive and immersive digital media and technologies to “convey not just the appearance but also the meaning and significance of cultural artefacts and the associated social agency that designed and used them”** (Champion 2006, 188). Such digital media include 3D models of sculptures, architecture, and other artifacts. In accessing the materials of this collection, along with their metadata, site visitors can expand their knowledge of the ancient Mediterranean and its material culture. 

This collection also acts as a means to demonstrate the utility of virtual heritage methods for experiencing and studying ancient artifacts. Users can view and examine these objects at all angles from any internet-connected device, meaning that they **do not have to co-locate with an ancient object to interact with it and perceive its details**. This is a key advantage of virtual heritage, and while there is no replacing the experience of seeing the object in-person, virtual heritage efforts like those included in the 3D Ancients collection provide expanded access to cultural heritage, which could garner more interest in archaeology, and therefore more funding for continued research (Jablonka et al. 2002, 18).

The 3D Ancients collection is continuously adding more items within the scope of the ancient Mediterranean. Due to existing biases pertaining to which regions are most often represented in archaeological research, the collection items will mostly originate from the ancient Greek and Roman worlds. However, **the intended scope of this collection encompasses the whole of the Mediterranean**, from the strait of Gibraltar to the eastern shores.
